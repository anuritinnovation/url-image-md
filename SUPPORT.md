## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Why isn't my image loading?
- Verify the image URL is publicly accessible and starts with `https://`.
- Check your internet connection.
- Ensure the image type is supported by standard browsers (PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP).

### How does the Redirect URL work?
- Drag a field with destination URLs into the "Redirect URL" data role.
- Once mapped, clicking the image in your report will open the link in a new browser tab.
